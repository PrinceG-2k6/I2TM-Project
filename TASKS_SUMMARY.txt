2. MAIN IDEA & CORE OBJECTIVES
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
We are building an intelligent traffic signal system that monitors traffic density, detects congestion
and risky driving patterns, supports emergency green corridors, and gives dynamic traffic suggestions.

Main Focus:
  - Reduce congestion
  - Improve traffic flow
  - Prevent ambulance delay
  - Support emergency vehicle movement
  - Make signal decisions based on real-time traffic conditions
  - Provide definite countdown information to drivers to increase cooperation

4. MODULE-WISE SPECIFICATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Dataset Module
  - Tasks: Find Indian traffic videos/datasets, support vehicle detection, density, congestion, and lane movements.
  - Backup: Simulated real-time sensor and stream data generation.

B. Traffic Density Module
  - Goal: Calculate how crowded each road/lane/junction side is (North, South, East, West).
  - Inputs: Vehicle count, road area/capacity, average speed.
  - Outputs: Low / Medium / High / Critical density %, vehicle count per direction, signal delta.

C. Congestion Alert Module
  - Goal: Generate alert when traffic is becoming heavy or blocked.
  - Outputs: Normal, Warning, Congested, Critical congestion with suggested actions.
  - Dashboard display: Junction name, alert level, reason, suggested timing adjustments.

D. Pattern Recognition Module (Cut-Maarna / Risky Driving)
  - Goal: Detect risky driving patterns from vehicle movement.
  - Patterns: Zig-zag movement, sudden swerving (>30°), abrupt lane cutting, wrong-side approach.
  - Outputs: Risky movement detected, vehicle ID, risk score (0-100), alert logged.

E. Lane Correction Module (Research Feature)
  - Goal: Detect vehicles moving outside lane boundaries, blocking turning lanes, or blocking emergency corridor paths.

F. Green Corridor Module (Emergency Triage)
  - Goal: Prevent ambulance delay by dynamically manipulating signals and clearing paths.
  - Patient Severity Levels:
      * Critical Patient: Highest priority, immediate green corridor (Triage RED, 90s countdown, hold conflicting lanes).
      * Serious Patient: High priority, faster signal clearance (Triage YELLOW, 60s countdown).
      * Stable Patient: Normal emergency support, priority if congestion risk is high (Triage GREEN, 30s countdown).
  - Roadside TV / Display Board Concept:
      * Use digital LED screens on traffic poles / advertisement displays.
      * Example: "Ambulance approaching. Keep left lane clear. Wait time: 90 seconds."
      * Driver Psychology: Drivers knowing the exact wait time are 84% more likely to cooperate and clear lanes.

G. Dynamic Traffic Suggestion Module
  - Goal: Suggest best action based on current traffic conditions (extend green for congested side, hold conflicting side for ambulance, roadside warning broadcast).

H. Dashboard Module
  - Owner: Sparsh, Suraj
  - Style: Clean SaaS aesthetic inspired by Stanza design references (warm cream #FFF8F0, coral #F97316, dark navy sidebar #121624, rounded cards, right filter drawer).
  - Sections: Dedicated non-scrolling tab views (Overview, FYIs, Features, Guards, Services, Timeline, Settings).


5. MVP PRIORITY & DEMO FLOW
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Traffic video or simulated junction feed inputs.
Step 2: System detects vehicles and calculates density on 4 approaches.
Step 3: Dashboard shows traffic density as Low / Medium / High / Critical.
Step 4: If density is high, system generates congestion alert and dynamic signal suggestion.
Step 5: Ambulance case enters system with patient severity (Critical/Serious/Stable).
Step 6: Emergency triage status updates (RED / YELLOW / GREEN).
Step 7: Green corridor manipulates signal timing to hold conflicting lanes and grant continuous green.
Step 8: Roadside TV/Display shows definite countdown (e.g. 90s wait) with driver instructions.
Step 9: Ambulance passes through cleared path.
Step 10: Signal returns to normal adaptive cycle.
Step 11: Risky driving / Cut-maarna alerts detect erratic trajectories with risk scores.
